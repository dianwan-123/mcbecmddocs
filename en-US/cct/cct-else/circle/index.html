How to draw a circle

Note: Document from senxyzplayeE.

How do you draw a circle in Bedrock Edition Minecraft? Let's use the new /execute command as an example to explain one approach. This method is known in the Bedrock Edition Minecraft command community as the "/execute nested dot-matrix circle".

Concept: To generate a circular shape, we can exploit the theorem that "the inscribed angle is half the central angle subtending the same arc." We repeatedly halve the angle while distributing points to different positions along the arc.

Let A be the center of the circle, and BC be entities at the two ends of a diameter. Let D be any point on the circle. By extending outward from A in the directions of CD and BD by a distance equal to the radius, we arrive at points E and F respectively. It is easy to see that these are the midpoints of arcs CD and BD.

Next, apply the same operation to points E and F as was done with point D. This yields four new points H, J, G, and I. It is easy to verify that regardless of where the starting point D is located, the angular spacing between these four points is always 45 degrees.

Continuing this nesting process, more and more points appear on the circle, and they are always evenly distributed.

The detailed commands are as follows. a is the center of the circle, and r refers to two entities placed at the two ends of a diameter. Note that these entities must be placed at a distance exactly equal to R from the center a; otherwise the generated circle will be unevenly distributed.

The portion enclosed in curly braces {} is the nested part. Repeat this segment multiple times — the more repetitions, the denser the generated points.

Note that the nested portion contains no rotation arithmetic and has no relationship to the x, y, z coordinates. This means we only need to change the starting position to change the orientation of the dot-matrix circle.

Tilt the armor stand by an angle — remembering to ensure the distance from the center remains R — and then adjust the ry rotation to tilt the circle upright. This modification allows you to generate a tilted circle.

It is foreseeable that, given proper placement, circles of any orientation can be generated.

However, this command has a limitation: it can only generate perfect circles, not oblique ellipses. What if we could draw truly arbitrary-orientation circles and ellipses?
