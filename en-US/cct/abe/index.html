Algebraic Expression of CCT

Authors: 1maginer0, senxyzplayeE

If a point P in Minecraft has coordinates (x, y, z) with facing angles (ry, rx), and needs to be represented in a mathematical 3D coordinate system, the transformation is (x, y, z) \to (z, x, y) and (ry, rx) \to (-ry, -rx). All coordinates and angles below follow the Minecraft convention.

Algebraic expressions are only applicable for theoretical analysis. Practical use must also consider floating-point precision, various game mechanics, etc. This article does not cover those.

Let the current execution position of the execute command be (x, y, z) and the execution facing be (ry, rx). The positioned transformations below only change the execution position, not the facing.

positioned a b c#

(x, y, z) \mapsto (a, b, c)

positioned ~a ~b ~c#

(x, y, z) \mapsto (x + a,\ y + b,\ z + c)

positioned ^a ^b ^c#

(x, y, z) \mapsto (x, y, z) + (a, b, c) \times \mathbf{R}^T

where \mathbf{R} is the rotation matrix:

\mathbf{R} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos(ry) & -\sin(rx)\sin(ry) & -\cos(rx)\sin(ry) \\ 0 & \cos(rx) & -\sin(rx) \\ \sin(ry) & \sin(rx)\cos(ry) & \cos(rx)\cos(ry) \end{bmatrix}

In non-matrix form:

x \mapsto x + a\cos(ry) - b\sin(rx)\sin(ry) - c\cos(rx)\sin(ry)
y \mapsto y + b\cos(rx) - c\sin(rx)
z \mapsto z + a\sin(ry) + b\sin(rx)\cos(ry) + c\cos(rx)\cos(ry)

The facing and rotated transformations below only change the facing, not the execution position.

facing a b c#

The resulting trigonometric values of ry and rx:

\sin ry = \frac{a - x}{\sqrt{(a-x)^2 + (c-z)^2}}
\cos ry = \frac{c - z}{\sqrt{(a-x)^2 + (c-z)^2}}
\sin rx = \frac{b - y}{\sqrt{(a-x)^2 + (b-y)^2 + (c-z)^2}}
\cos rx = \frac{\sqrt{(a-x)^2 + (c-z)^2}}{\sqrt{(a-x)^2 + (b-y)^2 + (c-z)^2}}

facing ~a ~b ~c#

The resulting trigonometric values of ry and rx:

\sin ry = \frac{a}{\sqrt{a^2 + c^2}}
\cos ry = \frac{c}{\sqrt{a^2 + c^2}}
\sin rx = \frac{b}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2 + c^2}}
\cos rx = \frac{\sqrt{a^2 + c^2}}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2 + c^2}}

Note: facing x+a y+b z+c = facing ~a ~b ~c, and this can also handle mixed cases.

facing ^a ^b ^c#

The resulting trigonometric values of ry and rx:

\sin(ry) = -\frac{a\cos(ry) - (b\sin(rx) + c\cos(rx))\sin(ry)}{\sqrt{a^2 + (b\sin(rx) + c\cos(rx))^2}}
\cos(ry) = \frac{a\sin(ry) + (b\sin(rx) + c\cos(rx))\cos(ry)}{\sqrt{a^2 + (b\sin(rx) + c\cos(rx))^2}}
\sin(rx) = \frac{c\sin(rx) - b\cos(rx)}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2 + c^2}}
\cos(rx) = \frac{\sqrt{a^2 + (b\sin(rx) + c\cos(rx))^2}}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2 + c^2}}

Partial conversion formulas for facing ^a ^b ^c:#

Formula 1:

facing ^a ^b ^c = rotated ~ ~ α facing ^a ^ r

\text{where } \alpha = -\arctan\!\left(\frac{b}{c}\right),\quad r = \sqrt{b^2 + c^2}

Formula 2:

rotated ~ ~ (-θ) facing ^a ^ ^b = facing ^a ^ p ^ q

\text{where } p = b\sin\theta,\quad q = b\cos\theta

rotated ~a ~b#

The resulting trigonometric values of ry and rx:

\sin(ry) = \sin(ry)\cos(a) + \cos(ry)\sin(a)
\cos(ry) = \cos(ry)\cos(a) - \sin(ry)\sin(a)
\sin(rx) = \sin(rx)\cos(b) + \cos(rx)\sin(b)
\cos(rx) = \cos(rx)\cos(b) - \sin(rx)\sin(b)

For rotated a b: this is not commonly seen in execute commands. The authors consider the derivation straightforward and have omitted it.

Special Note#

Pay attention to degree-to-radian conversion. This type of error is frequent in angle/radian calculations.

Appendix: Given computed values of \sin(ry), \cos(ry), \sin(rx), \cos(rx), to recover the in-game angles ry and rx, where \text{atan2} returns radians:

ry = \text{atan2}(\sin(ry),\ \cos(ry)) \times \frac{180}{\pi}
rx = \text{atan2}(\sin(rx),\ \cos(rx)) \times \frac{180}{\pi}

Algebraic Expressions (Vector Form)#

Define the current execution position vector \mathbf{P} (the coordinates of the current execute command), the rotation matrix \mathbf{R} = [\mathbf{L}\ \mathbf{U}\ \mathbf{F}], and \mathbf{Y} = (0, 1, 0).

\mathbf{R} = \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{L} & \mathbf{U} & \mathbf{F} \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos ry & -\sin rx \sin ry & -\cos rx \sin ry \\ 0 & \cos rx & -\sin rx \\ \sin ry & \sin rx \cos ry & \cos rx \cos ry \end{bmatrix}

Where:

  • \mathbf{L} points in the left-hand direction. Corresponds to Left.
  • \mathbf{U} points toward the top of the head, i.e., upward. Corresponds to Up.
  • \mathbf{F} points in the line-of-sight direction, i.e., forward. Corresponds to Front.

The vectors \mathbf{L}, \mathbf{U}, \mathbf{F} are all unit vectors, mutually perpendicular, and satisfy the cross-product relation \mathbf{L} = \mathbf{U} \times \mathbf{F}.

All coordinates and facings below follow the Minecraft convention. The positioned transformations below only change the execution position, not the facing.

positioned a b c#

\mathbf{P} \to (a, b, c)

positioned ~a ~b ~c#

\mathbf{P} \to \mathbf{P} + (a, b, c)

positioned ^a ^b ^c#

\mathbf{P} \to \mathbf{P} + a\mathbf{L} + b\mathbf{U} + c\mathbf{F}

The facing and rotated transformations below only change the facing, not the execution position.

facing — reference table for \mathbf{v}:#

Facing commandValue of \mathbf{v}
a b c(a, b, c) - \mathbf{P}
~a ~b ~c(a, b, c)
^a ^b ^ca\mathbf{L} + b\mathbf{U} + c\mathbf{F}

Then update \mathbf{F}, \mathbf{U}, \mathbf{L}:

\mathbf{F} \to \frac{\mathbf{v}}{\|\mathbf{v}\|}
\mathbf{U} \to \frac{\mathbf{Y} - (\mathbf{Y} \cdot \mathbf{F})\mathbf{F}}{\|\mathbf{Y} - (\mathbf{Y} \cdot \mathbf{F})\mathbf{F}\|}
\mathbf{L} \to \mathbf{U} \times \mathbf{F}

rotated#

Define M(a, b):

M(a, b) = \begin{bmatrix} \cos(a) & -\sin(b)\sin(a) & -\cos(b)\sin(a) \\ 0 & \cos(b) & -\sin(b) \\ \sin(a) & \sin(b)\cos(a) & \cos(b)\cos(a) \end{bmatrix}

rotated ~a ~b#

\Delta\mathbf{R} = M(a, b)
\mathbf{R} \to \mathbf{R} \cdot \Delta\mathbf{R}

rotated a b#

\mathbf{R}_{\text{goal}} = M(a, b)
\mathbf{R} \to \mathbf{R}_{\text{goal}}

Appendix: Rodrigues' Rotation Formula#

The rotation axis \mathbf{k} must be a unit vector.

\mathbf{v}' = \mathbf{v}\cos\theta + (\mathbf{k} \times \mathbf{v})\sin\theta + \mathbf{k}(\mathbf{k} \cdot \mathbf{v})(1 - \cos\theta)

An equivalent form:

\mathbf{v}' = \mathbf{v} + (\mathbf{k} \times \mathbf{v})\sin\theta + \mathbf{k} \times (\mathbf{k} \times \mathbf{v})(1 - \cos\theta)
